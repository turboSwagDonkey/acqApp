#   - alp4lib  -> needs the Vialux "ALP-4.2 high-speed API" folder. acqApp finds
#                 it via the registry, ACQAPP_ALP_DIR, or the standalone
#                 dmdGUI_project's dmd_config.json — see devices/dmd/alp.py.
